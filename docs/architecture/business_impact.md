# Business Impact & Pipeline Velocity Architecture

## Subsystem Architecture

The Business Impact subsystem connects GEO optimization outcomes with sales pipeline velocity, CRM deal stages, and revenue attribution models (First-Touch, Multi-Touch, AI-Linear).

## Components

1. **Revenue Impact Card**: Breakdown across GEO, SEO, and Paid Search.
2. **Pipeline Stage Velocity**: Tracking deal count and pipeline value across Discovery, PoC, and Legal stages.
3. **Multi-Touch Journey Flow**: Step-by-step buyer journey credit weighting.
