# Phase F6 AI Search & Governance Walkthrough

## User Flow

1. Open **AI Search Intelligence Platform** (`/dashboard/ai-search`).
2. Run prompt tracking search queries in the **AI Query Inspector**.
3. Compare Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude in the **AI Models Matrix**.
4. Explore business entities and directed relationships in the **Knowledge Graph**.
5. Check multi-region visibility and auto-fix GEO recommendations in the **Heatmap**.
6. Manage AI safety policies and SOC2 compliance scorecards in **AI Governance**.
